THE DEVOTED MC MISSION STATEMENT

The Devoted is a non territorial, non discriminating, family and community oriented motorcycle club. We are devoted first to each other as brothers, and secondly, we are devoted to the motorcycle lifestyle. Family is of the utmost importance to us. We will keep our club family oriented at all costs. Supporting our local communities is only a small part of what we are as a club. We are about social gathering, as well as putting our wheels to the pavement. Another major aspect of our club will be protecting, and defending the rights that our forefathers laid down their lives to give us. We vow to conduct ourselves with honor, integrity, commitment, and loyalty to this lifestyle and to follow the ethics expected of a club within the motorcycle community. We are proud of who we are and wouldn't change it for anyone..... We are THE DEVOTED.
